About the Project

Team 9 developed a Messenger App as part of a Scrum-based software engineering project. The application supports communication between students and teaching assistants through features such as login, private messaging, group messaging, broadcast announcements, online user visibility, and message indicators. Throughout the course, our team applied Scrum practices, including sprint planning, daily standups, and iterative development.
